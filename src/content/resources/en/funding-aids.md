## Why this matters before you ask for a quote

Governments at the national, regional, and EU level run active subsidy programs that can cover **part or all** of your digital project — websites, e-commerce, custom apps, even strategic consulting. The amounts are not trivial: depending on your business size and region, a program can cover **€2,000 to €24,000+** without dilution, equity, or repayment in many cases.

**Before locking in a budget, check whether you qualify.** Many of my clients discover they're eligible for a subsidy *after* they've committed to a quote, and adjusting mid-project is messier than designing the project around the subsidy from day one.

This guide lists the programs I see most often in client briefs, organized by region. Eligibility, deadlines, and amounts change — always confirm on the official portal linked in each section.

## Spain 🇪🇸

The most active subsidy landscape of any country in my client base. If you're a Spanish autónomo or PYME, the odds you qualify for *something* are high.

### Kit Digital

The flagship national program for digital transformation, managed by Red.es / Acelera Pyme.

- **Who qualifies:** Autónomos, micro-businesses (<10 employees), and SMEs (<50 employees) registered in Spain.
- **How much:** €2,000 – €12,000 depending on company segment.
- **What it covers (categories relevant to my services):**
  - Web presence + basic SEO — up to **€2,000** for autónomos
  - E-commerce / online store
  - Customer management (CRM)
  - Cybersecurity
  - Business intelligence and analytics
  - Electronic invoicing
- **How it works:** You apply for a digital voucher (*bono digital*), then hire a registered **Agente Digitalizador** to implement the work. The agent bills directly against the voucher up to the cap — you don't pay out of pocket within that limit.
- **Combining with my services:** Ask me about my current Agente Digitalizador status when you reach out. If I'm registered for your category, the voucher covers my work directly. If not, you can still use Kit Digital with a registered agent for the basic categories, and hire me on top for the parts outside the program scope (custom design, integrations, mobile apps, etc.).

📌 Official portal: [acelerapyme.gob.es/kit-digital](https://www.acelerapyme.gob.es/kit-digital)

### Kit Consulting

A newer program (active since 2024) focused on **advanced digital consulting** rather than implementation.

- **Who qualifies:** SMEs in Spain with 10–249 employees.
- **How much:** Up to **€24,000** across multiple advisory categories.
- **Covers:** AI for business strategy, advanced cybersecurity, digital transformation strategy, data analytics, process automation, sales 4.0.
- **Best use case:** If your project requires strategic discovery *before* development ("should we build a custom Flutter app or use a SaaS?"), Kit Consulting may cover that scoping phase.

📌 Official portal: [acelerapyme.gob.es/kit-consulting](https://www.acelerapyme.gob.es/kit-consulting)

### Regional grants (subvenciones autonómicas)

Each autonomous community in Spain runs its own digitalization grants on top of Kit Digital. These usually open 1–2 times per year, with amounts and eligibility varying widely.

- **Galicia** — Xunta de Galicia / IGAPE digitalization aids
- **Cataluña** — Generalitat de Catalunya, *Cupons d'Innovació* and Accelera el Creixement
- **Madrid** — Comunidad de Madrid, ICEX-NEXT and related programs
- **Andalucía** — Junta de Andalucía, *Bono Digital* and related
- **Valencia, País Vasco, Aragón, Castilla y León, Canarias** — each with its own annual calls

📌 Central directory of all regional and national aids: [acelerapyme.gob.es](https://www.acelerapyme.gob.es/) → *"Ayudas para tu empresa"*

### ENISA (soft loans, not grants)

If your project is closer to a startup than an SME modernization, **ENISA** runs *participative loans* with grace periods and minimal real guarantees.

- **Amount:** €25,000 – €1,500,000 across three lines (Jóvenes Emprendedores, Emprendedores, Crecimiento).
- **Best for:** Companies with existing revenue or a clear path to it. Not pure pre-seed ideas.

📌 Official portal: [enisa.es](https://www.enisa.es/)

### CDTI (R&D innovation funding)

If your project has a genuine technical R&D component — novel algorithms, scientific innovation, advanced tech stacks beyond standard development — **CDTI** offers a mix of grants and soft loans.

- **Best for:** R&D-heavy projects. Not standard web or app development.

📌 Official portal: [cdti.es](https://www.cdti.es/)

## European Union 🇪🇺

EU programs are competitive (not voucher-based like Kit Digital) and typically require a real application effort. They're worth pursuing for larger or more innovative projects.

### EIC Accelerator

The European Innovation Council's flagship SME instrument.

- **Who:** Innovative SMEs and small mid-caps with breakthrough technology.
- **How much:** Up to **€2.5M in grant** + up to **€15M in equity** under Blended Finance.
- **Bar:** Very high — typically below 5% acceptance rate.

📌 Official portal: [eic.ec.europa.eu](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en)

### Digital Europe Programme

Funds large-scale deployment of digital tech (HPC, AI, cybersecurity, advanced digital skills) across member states.

- **Who:** Mostly consortia of universities, large enterprises, and public bodies. Solo SMEs rarely apply directly.
- **Best for:** When you're part of a broader transformation initiative led by a larger entity.

📌 Official portal: [digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/activities/digital-programme)

### Horizon Europe

Massive research-and-innovation programme. Mostly geared at research consortia — less relevant for standard web or app projects, but worth knowing if your work crosses into research.

📌 Official portal: [research-and-innovation.ec.europa.eu/.../horizon-europe](https://research-and-innovation.ec.europa.eu/funding/funding-opportunities/funding-programmes-and-open-calls/horizon-europe_en)

## Other regions

I don't have first-hand experience deploying subsidies outside Spain and the EU. If you're elsewhere, these are the entry points worth checking:

- **United Kingdom** — [Innovate UK](https://iuk-business-connect.org.uk/) grants, SEIS/EIS investor tax credits
- **United States** — [SBIR / STTR](https://www.sbir.gov/) federal grants, state-level economic-development programs
- **Mexico / LATAM** — Varies country by country; check your national economic-ministry portal

If you find a program in your region and want help structuring a project to fit its eligibility, reach out and I'll work within those constraints.

## How to combine a subsidy with my services

Three patterns work in practice:

1. **The subsidy covers the core, I do the extras.** Kit Digital covers your basic website through a registered Agente Digitalizador; you hire me directly for the parts outside the program — custom design, advanced features, integrations, mobile apps.

2. **I handle everything via the subsidy** — only when I'm registered as Agente Digitalizador for your category. The voucher pays me directly; you don't disburse cash up to the voucher cap.

3. **Subsidy + cash mix.** For larger projects — say a full Flutter app at €10,000 — Kit Digital covers a slice (€2,000) and you cover the rest. We split the invoicing accordingly.

Which pattern fits depends on your business segment, region, and project scope. When you reach out, include:

- Your business segment (autónomo / micro / SME)
- Your country and, if Spain, your autonomous community
- Roughly what you want to build

I'll tell you which subsidies look like a fit, and we'll size the scope to land inside them where it makes sense.
